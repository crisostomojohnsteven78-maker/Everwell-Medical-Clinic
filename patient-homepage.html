Inside Everwell

Built to feel less like a clinic.

Every consult room, the pharmacy counter, and the lab sit on one floor and one system — so your visit moves forward instead of backtracking through paperwork.

Private consultation rooms

Quiet, one-on-one rooms for every specialty — no shared curtains, no rushed conversations.

Partner pharmacy network

Your pharmacist reviews and clears every prescription in-clinic, then routes it straight to a trusted partner pharmacy through CPOE — no separate errand to explain your case again.

Partner labs & imaging centers

Orders and samples are logged at Everwell and routed to accredited partner labs and imaging centers through our LIS integration, with results synced back to your chart automatically.

Patient lounge

A calm waiting area away from the clinical bustle — water, seating that isn't a hard bench, and no crowding.

One shared health record

Doctors, pharmacists, and lab staff all read from the same file, so you don't repeat your history at every desk.

Hospital referral network

When a case needs more than outpatient care, your vitals and notes go ahead of you to our partner hospitals.
